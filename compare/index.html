Paper texture apps for Mac

Vendor-described features; not independently tested. Official sources checked October 14, 2026. This comparison is written by Parchmatte’s maker.

Parchmatte

Paper texture for one window or the whole screen, adjustable strength and softness, and optional Page Light with separate Light Strength and Glow controls.

Parchmatte features · October 14, 2026

Paperman

The vendor describes paper textures across displays, app exclusions and display selection. Its Desk Lamp mode adds a light effect.

PaperScreen

The vendor describes Color E-paper textures and a separate Soft Light mode, with schedules, display choice and app exclusions.

PaperScreen official details · October 14, 2026

Deckle

The project describes a click-through paper-grain overlay with paper presets and display controls.
