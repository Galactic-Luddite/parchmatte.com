Parchmatte vs Night Shift on Mac

Night Shift is built into macOS and costs nothing. On a schedule, it moves every colour on the display toward orange, with one slider for how far. The screen stays exactly as glossy, and it can't skip an app or a window.

Parchmatte leaves your colours alone unless you turn on Page Light. You can run both. The grain sits on top of whatever tint Night Shift is applying.

Pick Night Shift if all you want is warmer colour at night. It's already on your Mac.

Parchmatte vs f.lux

f.lux is free and runs on far more than Macs. It works out a bedtime from your wake-up time and warms the screen in steps through the evening, and it can switch itself off for a chosen app. It's a colour tool, full stop: no texture, no glare reduction. Its own update page lists 42.2, from June 2023, as the latest Mac release.

Page Light is simpler: seven tints and a strength slider. The grain does the part f.lux was never meant to do. The two stack, though most people end up choosing one tint or the other.

Pick f.lux if you want the most control over colour temperature by time of day, or the same tool on Windows and Linux.

Parchmatte vs Paperman: a Paperman alternative with a warm tint

Paperman is the better-known of the two, and it's good. Nine textures to our seven, a Windows version, and a lamp-pool mode for evenings. It can exclude apps or run only in the ones you choose, but the smallest thing it can paper is an app. Its Mac App Store build can't cover full-screen apps; the direct download can.

What it doesn't have is a warm tint or a single-window mode. Page Light has its own strength and schedule, so evenings don't need a second app. ⌃⌥P papers just the front window, and the paper follows it as it moves, resizes, switches tabs and goes full screen. Everything else on screen stays as it was. Parchmatte is also open source under the MIT licence: build it yourself for nothing, or pay $2.99 on the App Store for a signed copy with automatic updates. Paperman is a one-time purchase, $9.99 on the US App Store when we checked.

Pick Paperman if you want the most textures, or you also work on Windows.

Questions

I already use Night Shift. Do I still need this?

Only if the glassy look bothers you. Night Shift warms the colours and leaves the surface as it is. Parchmatte adds a paper grain on top, and you can keep Night Shift running underneath it.

Does it turn my screen orange?

Not unless you ask it to. The grain on its own doesn't change colour. Page Light is a separate, optional warm tint with its own strength control and schedule.

How is this different from Paperman?

Both lay a paper texture over the screen. Paperman has more textures and a Windows version. Parchmatte adds an optional warm tint, can paper a single window instead of a whole app, and is open source: build it free or pay $2.99 on the Mac App Store for a signed copy.

Can I paper only one window?

Yes. Press ⌃⌥P and the front window gets its own paper, which follows it as it moves, resizes, switches tabs and goes full screen. f.lux and Paperman work per app, not per window.

Is it really free?

Yes. The source is MIT licensed and builds in a few minutes with Apple's free developer tools. The $2.99 App Store version is the same app, signed and updated for you.
