Parchmatte / Guide · Parchmatte 1.3

Add paper texture to one Mac window.

Give a writing or reading window its own paper texture while keeping the rest of your workspace as it is. Here is how to set it up from Parchmatte’s menu bar controls.

You will need Parchmatte open on a Mac running macOS 13 or later. It is available on the Mac App Store.

Put paper on your chosen window.

  1. Turn off whole-screen paper. Open Parchmatte’s controls in the menu bar. If Paper Whole Screen is checked, select it to turn it off. This lets you see paper on individual windows.

  2. Bring your window to the front. Click the window you want to paper, then open Parchmatte’s controls again.

  3. Choose the window action. Select Paper [app name] Window. The controls use your app’s name: for example, Paper TextEdit Window.

  4. Adjust that window’s paper. The controls switch to This Window — [app name] as soon as you add the paper. Choose Texture, adjust Strength for the amount of texture, and adjust Softness for the grain’s softness.

Choose from nine textures in Texture, including Velvet, a fine plush texture in Wine. Use This Window for the selected window. Screen and New Windows sets the whole-screen look and the starting style for new window covers; windows you have already styled keep their own settings. Window styles last for the current app session.

Clicks, scrolling and typing pass through the overlay, so you can keep using the window underneath.

Add optional Page Light.

Select This Window, then open Page Light. Under Color, choose one of six single-color lights or Color Phase, a still blend of multiple colors. Use Light Strength for the amount of light, Glow for how it fades toward the edges, Shape for Oval, Round, Wide or Tall, and Position to place it anywhere: drag the dot, or click an area to jump to a corner, an edge or the center. Glow offers Diffuse, Balanced and Focused.

Choose Off in Texture to use light alone; the texture and its adjustments are kept. Select the named texture again to bring it back. On a narrow layout, use Back to return to the texture controls. Set Light Strength to 0% for no light while keeping the chosen light preset, or choose Off under Color in Page Light for texture alone.

Remove the paper when you are finished.

Bring the papered window to the front, open Parchmatte’s controls, and choose Remove Paper from [app name] Window. To clear all individual window papers, choose Remove Window Papers; the number beside it shows how many have been added.

You can also press Control + Option + P with the window in front to add or remove its paper. If the shortcut is already in use, use the controls action.

If you do not see the window’s paper

Check that Paper Whole Screen is off and that the window you want is in front. The window action is disabled when Parchmatte cannot find a window to target. The status at the top of the controls can also show whether Parchmatte is paused by snooze, its schedule, battery settings or an excluded app.
