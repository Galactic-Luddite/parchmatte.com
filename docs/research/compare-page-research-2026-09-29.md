## Claims we can safely make (citation to reuse)

1. Night Shift and f.lux change colour temperature; neither adds texture or addresses glare/contrast. [1], [5]
2. Night Shift has no per-app control and applies to the whole display. [2] (absence), [25]
3. Night Shift offers sunset-to-sunrise, custom hours, and a manual "until" toggle. [2]
4. Night Shift requires macOS 10.12.4+ and is free with macOS. [1]
5. f.lux is free for macOS and is not open source. [5], [12]
6. f.lux can disable itself for a chosen app (per-app), schedules by location and wake time, and offers Daytime/Sunset/Bedtime modes. [6]
7. f.lux's own FAQ says it is not for advanced colour work and lists fullscreen-video and DDC/CI monitor issues. [8]
8. f.lux's most recent Mac release on its own update page is v42.2 (June 2023). [7] (phrase as "as of [date]")
9. Running f.lux and Night Shift together stacks the two tints. [13]
10. Paperman is a paper-grain overlay, requires macOS 12+, is a paid one-time purchase, and is not open source. [16], [15]
11. Paperman's Mac App Store version cannot overlay full-screen apps; DoublOne offers a direct download that can. [19], [15]
12. Paperman offers per-app exclusion/allowlist, day/night and sunrise/sunset scheduling, and optional hotkeys. [16], [14]
13. Paperman states it works alongside colour filters such as Night Shift. [14]
14. A texture overlay and a colour-temperature filter address different things and can be combined. [14]

## Claims to avoid

- Any Kelvin figure for Night Shift (Apple publishes none; "2700-3000K" is a competitor's estimate [25]).
- "f.lux removes 4-5x more blue light than Night Shift" as our own claim: it is f.lux staff's 2017 statement [10]; quote and attribute if used at all.
- A minimum macOS for f.lux (official pages are silent).
- "f.lux is abandoned/unmaintained": only state the last listed Mac release date [7].
- "Night Shift is broken on macOS 26": one forum report [27]; say "some users report" with the link, or omit.
- A single fixed Paperman price: it varies $5.99-$11.99 by channel/promo [15], [16], [20], [21]. Use "US App Store price as of <date>".
- "Paperman crashes / blanks screenshots": seen only in a search snippet, not in fetched reviews (UNVERIFIED).
- "Paperman has no hotkeys" (it has optional ones [16]) or "no schedule" (it has [16]).
- Any Paperman release dated 2024 (see year artefact note).
- Health outcomes (sleep, eye-strain relief) for any product, including Parchmatte; Wikipedia [12] notes neither Night Shift nor f.lux has strong evidence. Phrase as comfort/preference.
- Per-window control for f.lux or Paperman: both are per-app only [6], [16]; Parchmatte's per-window claim is a differentiator but must be verifiable in the README.

## Naming caution (Q9)

- Every competitor page found (iMore, nightshiftkeeper.com, circadianshield.com, Digital Trends) names Apple, Night Shift, f.lux and Paperman plainly in titles and body copy; none uses logos. This is ordinary nominative use. [13], [23], [25], [21]
- Nominative fair use test (three prongs): product not identifiable without the mark; use no more of the mark than needed; nothing suggesting endorsement or affiliation. Comparative claims must be "strictly accurate and supported by backup documentation". [31]
- Apple's guidelines [4]: word marks only, never the Apple logo or product icons; do not use marks in possessive or plural (write "Night Shift on macOS", not "Apple's Night Shift"); referential phrases such as "compatible with" / "for use with" / "runs on" are permitted; the Apple mark should be less prominent than our product name; include an attribution line such as "Mac, macOS and Night Shift are trademarks of Apple Inc." in the footer.
- Obvious no-gos: Apple, f.lux or Paperman logos/icons; a page title that reads as their page (keep "Parchmatte vs ..." ordering); unsourced negative claims (crashes, abandonment); reusing Paperman texture names as if ours; screenshots of their UI (prefer none).
- "Paperman" carries a ™ on paperman.cc [14]; use plain text "Paperman" and a trademark notice line. f.lux registration status not checked (**UNVERIFIED**).

## Synthesis

The colour-temperature comparison space (f.lux vs Night Shift) is saturated with 2026-dated vendor pages, all silent on glare and texture. The texture space has product pages but no comparison page at all, and Parchmatte is absent from AlternativeTo where "Paperman alternative" searches land. The strongest positioning is "texture and tint are different layers": Night Shift/f.lux only shift colour, Paperman only textures (plus a lamp effect), Parchmatte does both, is MIT, cheapest, and is the only one claiming per-window control. The main factual hazards are moving Paperman prices, the Paperman full-screen caveat (App Store build only), and stale f.lux release info.
