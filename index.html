Light, with or without paper.

Choose one of six single-color Page Lights or Color Phase, a still blend of multiple colors. Adjust Light Strength and choose a Diffuse, Balanced or Focused glow. Shape the light with Oval, Round, Wide or Tall, and place it anywhere: drag it on a small picture of your screen, or click to jump to a corner, an edge or the center.

Find your texture.

Choose from nine textures: Parchmatte, Fine Grain, Chalkboard, Woven, Imprint, Soft Leaf, Felt, Denim and Velvet. Adjust texture strength, softness and orientation. Turn texture off to use Page Light on its own, then bring the texture back with its settings preserved.

Right in your menu bar.

Select This Window to edit that window’s saved style. Screen and New Windows sets the whole-screen look and the starting style for new window covers. Windows you have already styled keep their own settings. The status above the controls describes the current effect separately from your editing target. A few practical details

What is new in Parchmatte 1.3?

Use Page Light without paper texture, or try Color Phase, a still blend of multiple colors. Glow styles are now named Diffuse, Balanced and Focused. Set Light Strength to 0% for no light while keeping your selected preset. Existing saved light keeps its appearance, although the displayed percentage may change. Place Page Light anywhere by dragging it on a small picture of your screen. The redesigned controls use familiar Mac switches, menus and sliders, and list every keyboard shortcut under App & Workspace.

Can two windows in the same app look different?

Yes. Each window with its own paper can have its own texture and light. Window choices are kept for the current app session; they are not saved as permanent per-app rules.

Does it make my Mac screen look like paper or an e-paper display?

It gives your screen a paper-like, matte look by drawing a fine texture, and an optional tint of light, over your windows. It changes how the screen looks, not the display itself: brightness and refresh rate stay the same.

Does it get in the way?

Clicks, scrolling and typing pass through the overlay. Adjust the paper from your menu bar while you keep using your apps.

Does it appear in screenshots?

By default, Parchmatte asks macOS to leave the overlay out of screenshots. Some capture tools may still include it.

What can Parchmatte see?

Parchmatte reads window positions and sizes to place its overlays. It does not read window contents, window titles or your typing, collect personal data, or make network connections.

Where can I get Parchmatte?

Parchmatte is available on the Mac App Store. The US price is $2.99; regional prices vary. Requires macOS 13 or later.
