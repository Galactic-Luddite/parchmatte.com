Paper texture for
your Mac’s screen.

Add a fine paper grain to your display, or just one window. Adjust the texture and add an optional Page Light tint.

Build from source

Free and open source · macOS 13 or later
Mac App Store · $2.99 · Coming soon

Make it your paper.

Choose from eight textures: Parchmatte, Fine Grain, Chalkboard, Woven, Imprint, Soft Leaf, Felt, and Denim. Set the strength and softness from the menu bar. Page Light adds a separate tint, with its own glow control.

Just the window you want.

Press ⌃⌥P to add paper to the front window. It follows as you move, resize, or go full screen. You can also cover the whole display.

On your schedule
Use sunrise and sunset, or set your own hours. Snooze for a break or pause on battery.
Across your workspace
Choose which displays to cover and which apps to exclude. Clicks and typing pass through.

Paper on a darker screen.

Woven adds a fine linen weave. Denim has a deeper, diagonal grain. Here they are on the same dark terminal demo, so you can compare the texture without a bright page underneath.

Already using Night Shift or f.lux?

You can keep either running and use Parchmatte for the texture. Compare Parchmatte, Night Shift, f.lux and Paperman.

A few practical details

Does it get in the way?

Clicks, scrolling and typing pass through the overlay. You can exclude apps, snooze Parchmatte, or pause it on battery.

Does it appear in screenshots?

By default, Parchmatte asks macOS to leave the overlay out of screenshots. Some capture tools may still include it.

What can Parchmatte see?

Parchmatte reads window positions and sizes to place its overlays. It does not read window contents or your typing, collect personal data, or make network connections.

Is it free?

The source code is free under the MIT license. You can build it with Apple's developer tools. A $2.99 Mac App Store version with automatic updates is coming soon.

What are the keyboard shortcuts?
KeysAction
⌃⌥OPaper the whole screen
⌃⌥PPaper the front window (again to remove)
⌃⌥↑ / ⌃⌥↓Stronger or lighter
⌃⌥← / ⌃⌥→Less or more softness
⌃⌥TNext texture
⌃⌥IFlip the grain’s direction
⌃⌥LNext Page Light tint
⌃⌥GNext lamp glow
⌃⌥SSnooze for 15 minutes, or resume

When the front window has its own paper, the arrow, texture, Page Light and glow keys adjust that window. Otherwise, they adjust the whole screen and new windows.
