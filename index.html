What it does

  • Paper over everything, or just one window. Covers follow windows as they move, resize, switch tabs and go full screen.
  • Seven textures: Parchmatte, Fine Grain, Chalkboard, Woven, Imprint, Soft Leaf and Felt, with a softness control.
  • Page Light tints from Candlelight to Aurora, with adjustable glow.
  • Schedules: sunset to sunrise, sunrise to sunset, or your own hours. Snooze, per-display control, excluded apps, pause on battery.
  • Never in the way: clicks, scrolling and typing pass straight through. By default it asks macOS to leave it out of screenshots, though some newer capture tools may still show it.

Hotkeys

KeysAction
⌃⌥OPaper the whole screen
⌃⌥PPaper the front window (again to remove)
⌃⌥↑ / ⌃⌥↓Stronger or lighter
⌃⌥← / ⌃⌥→Less or more softness
⌃⌥TNext texture
⌃⌥LNext Page Light tint
⌃⌥GNext lamp glow
⌃⌥SSnooze for 15 minutes, or resume

When the front window has its own paper, the arrow, texture, lamp and glow keys change just that window. Otherwise they change the whole screen and new windows.

Private by design

Parchmatte can't see what's on your screen and doesn't read your typing. It has no network code at all and collects nothing. It runs in Apple's App Sandbox with no special permissions. Privacy policy.

Questions

Is Parchmatte an alternative to Night Shift or f.lux?

Partly. Night Shift and f.lux only warm your screen's colour. Parchmatte adds a paper grain that takes the glassy edge off, and its warm Page Light tint is optional, so you can use it as a warm tint on its own or run it alongside either one. See the full comparison, including Paperman.

Does it slow my Mac down or get in the way?

No. It's a lightweight overlay: clicks, scrolling and typing pass straight through, and you can exclude apps or pause it on battery.

Can I paper just one window?

Yes. Press ⌃⌥P to paper the front window. The cover follows it as it moves, resizes, switches tabs and goes full screen.

Does it show up in screenshots?

By default it asks macOS to leave it out of screenshots, though some newer capture tools may still show it.

Is it really free?

Yes. The source is MIT licensed and builds with Apple's free tools. The $2.99 App Store version is the same app, signed and updated for you.

Build it yourself

xcode-select --install
git clone https://github.com/Galactic-Luddite/parchmatte.git
cd parchmatte
./scripts/build.sh
open dist/Parchmatte.app

Needs macOS 13 or later. Full instructions are in the README.
