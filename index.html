Make it your paper.

Choose from nine textures: Parchmatte, Fine Grain, Chalkboard, Woven, Imprint, Soft Leaf, Felt, Denim and Velvet. Velvet adds a fine plush texture in Wine. Adjust strength and softness from the menu bar, with separate settings for a single window or the whole screen.

Add a little light.

Choose from six Page Light colors and three glow styles. Shape the light with Oval, Round, Wide or Tall, then place it in the center, along an edge or in a corner. Adjust Light Strength separately from the paper, or turn Page Light off for texture alone.

A few practical details

What is new in Parchmatte 1.2.1?

Page Light has four shapes and nine positions, with centered Oval as the default. Customization controls stay open while you make changes. Turning a window’s paper off and back on restores its style during the current app session.

Can two windows in the same app look different?

Yes. Each window with its own paper can have its own texture and light. Window choices are kept for the current app session; they are not saved as permanent per-app rules.

Does it get in the way?

Clicks, scrolling and typing pass through the overlay. Adjust the paper from your menu bar while you keep using your apps.

Does it appear in screenshots?

By default, Parchmatte asks macOS to leave the overlay out of screenshots. Some capture tools may still include it.

What can Parchmatte see?

Parchmatte reads window positions and sizes to place its overlays. It does not read window contents, window titles or your typing, collect personal data, or make network connections.

Where can I get Parchmatte?

Parchmatte is available on the Mac App Store. The US price is $2.99; regional prices vary. Requires macOS 13 or later.
