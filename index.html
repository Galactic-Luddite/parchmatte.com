A few practical details

Does it get in the way?

Clicks, scrolling and typing pass through the overlay. You can exclude apps, snooze Parchmatte, or pause it on battery.

Does it appear in screenshots?

By default, Parchmatte asks macOS to leave the overlay out of screenshots. Some capture tools may still include it.

What can Parchmatte see?

Parchmatte reads window positions and sizes to place its overlays. It does not read window contents, window titles or your typing, collect personal data, or make network connections.

Where can I get Parchmatte?

Parchmatte is available on the Mac App Store. The US price is $2.99; regional prices vary. Requires macOS 13 or later.

What are the keyboard shortcuts?
KeysAction
⌃⌥OPaper the whole screen
⌃⌥PPaper the front window (again to remove)
⌃⌥↑ / ⌃⌥↓Stronger or lighter
⌃⌥← / ⌃⌥→Less or more softness
⌃⌥TNext texture
⌃⌥IFlip the grain’s direction
⌃⌥⇧↑ / ⌃⌥⇧↓Stronger or softer Page Light
⌃⌥LNext Page Light tint
⌃⌥GNext glow level
⌃⌥SSnooze for 15 minutes, or resume

When the front window has its own paper, the arrow, texture, Page Light, glow and light strength keys adjust that window. Otherwise, they adjust the whole screen and new windows.
